六级作文：先让步，再反驳

小黑英语 · 原创六级风格练习

01 看懂三句话

It is true that a part-time job may take time away from study.
However, a well-chosen job can teach students to manage their time better.
For example, a student who tutors twice a week has to plan revision in advance, and this habit helps when exams come.

分别标出：让步句、转折句、理由 + 例子。

02 改写一边倒的段落

Part-time jobs are good for students. They can earn money and gain experience. So students should take part-time jobs.

补一句让步，再用转折词转回来：

03 迁移练习

Some people believe online courses will replace classroom teaching.

写一句让步 + 一句转折（两句要说同一件事）：

参考答案

Admittedly, online courses are flexible and cheap. However, classroom teaching gives students instant feedback that a screen cannot fully replace.

检查：有一句让步 → 转折后是你的观点 → 让步和理由对准同一点。
