# 内容检查：六级作文 · 让步与反驳

| 项目 | 结论 |
| --- | --- |
| 题目 | 原创六级风格题目（大学生打工 vs 专心学习），未冒充真题 |
| 核心技巧 | 让步一句（It is true that / Admittedly / Some may argue that）→ 转折一句（However / Even so / But this does not mean that）→ 理由 + 例子 |
| 例句语法 | “It is true that a part-time job may take time away from study.” “However, a well-chosen job can teach students to manage their time better.” “For example, a student who tutors twice a week has to plan revision in advance, and this habit helps when exams come.” 均为完整句，搭配自然 |
| 使用边界 | 让步与反驳对准同一个点；让步一句即可；不必每段都用；未称其为万能模板，未承诺分数 |
| 练习答案 | Admittedly, online courses are flexible and cheap. However, classroom teaching gives students instant feedback that a screen cannot fully replace.（参考写法，表达不唯一） |
| 文风 | 旁白通过项目模板腔检查（无“不是……而是……”“别再……”“先别……”等） |
