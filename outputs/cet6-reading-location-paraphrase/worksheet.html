Cities are redesigning some public spaces to make them more resilient to extreme heat. Instead of adding more air-conditioning, planners are combining shade trees, reflective materials, and water-sensitive landscaping. These measures do not merely lower surface temperatures; they also encourage residents to spend more time outdoors.

Universities are rethinking orientation programs. Rather than filling the first week with formal talks, some schools are giving new students small team projects and guided visits around campus. The aim is to help them build connections before classes become demanding.

What is the main purpose of the new orientation programs?

How cities use several measures to cope with extreme heat.
