# CLUD / CLUS 内容准确性检查

核对日期：2026-10-13。状态：内容与来源已核对；成片感官终审另记在 review/REVIEW.md。release 保持 draft。

| 单词 | 视频中的现代义 | 词源关系 | 记忆桥梁是否注明为助记 | 权威来源 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| include | 包括、使成为整体的一部分 | Latin includere：in- + claudere | 收入餐盘并合盒；现代义不要求真实封闭 | [Cambridge](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/include)、[Etymonline](https://www.etymonline.com/word/include) |
| exclude | 排除、不包括、阻止参与 | Latin excludere：ex- + claudere | 合盒并把餐盘留在外面 | [Cambridge](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/exclude)、[Etymonline](https://www.etymonline.com/word/exclude) |
| conclude | 结束；推断、得出结论 | Latin concludere：com- 的同化形式 + claudere | 收齐资料并合文件夹；为助记，不是推断的定义 | [Cambridge](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/conclude)、[Etymonline](https://www.etymonline.com/word/conclude) |
| preclude | 阻止、使不可能 | Latin praecludere：prae- + claudere | 提前关闸门；现代义不要求主语有提前阻止的意图 | [Cambridge](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/english/preclude)、[Oxford](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/preclude)、[Etymonline](https://www.etymonline.com/word/preclude) |
| seclude | 使隔离、使独处 | Latin secludere：se- + claudere | 拉帘隔开阅读角；secluded 可描述僻静之地 | [Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/seclude)、[Etymonline](https://www.etymonline.com/word/seclude) |

拉丁复合词里可见 -cludere 与 -clusus，所以 CLUD / CLUS 归同一词根家族；include → inclusion 展示动词与名词词干关系。画面把前缀与 clude 分组用于教学拼写，不把结尾 e 称作有独立词义的后缀。

例句均为原创教学例句：The price includes/excludes breakfast；We concluded that the plan was workable；Heavy rain precluded outdoor activities；a secluded village。conclude that 后接完整句；preclude someone from doing something 的搭配经 Oxford 核对。

练习答案：The fee **excludes** meals；The injury **precluded** him from playing（选项为 concluded / precluded）。不使用 excluded / precluded 二选一，因为某些语境里 excluded him from playing 也可成立。答案页说明词义有交集，需看上下文。
