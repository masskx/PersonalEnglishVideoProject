03 独立完成迁移题

如今，许多大学生利用假期参加志愿活动，因为这些经历不仅能锻炼他们的沟通能力，也能让他们更好地了解社会。

参考译文与讲评

In recent years, an increasing number of young people have chosen to visit museums on weekends, because doing so helps them learn about local history and slow down their busy lives.

主干：越来越多的年轻人选择走进博物馆。。近年来用 in recent years；本版用现在完成时表达延续趋势；“走进博物馆”按语义处理为 visit museums；doing so 指代前面的选择。

Today, many college students take part in volunteer activities during their vacations, because these experiences improve their communication skills and help them better understand society.

检查：主干能单独成立；时间地点位置合理；动词搭配自然；并列形式一致。
